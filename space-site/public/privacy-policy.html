YOUR DATA, RESPECTED

Privacy Policy

This page explains what information may be handled when you visit Space, Subhadip Dey’s portfolio, and how the site uses it.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Information you choose to share

If you use the contact form, the site may collect your name, email address and message so Subhadip can respond. If you leave a project comment, your name and comment text may be stored and displayed with that project.

Chat and browser storage

If you use Space Bot, the text you send is processed to provide a reply. Depending on the site’s current configuration, a chat request may be handled by the site’s server and an enabled AI service. Do not send sensitive or confidential information.

When the site is running without its backend, some demo features may save contact messages, comments or image choices in your browser’s local storage. This information stays in that browser unless you clear its site data; it is not a reliable way to submit a message.

How information is used

Information is used to reply to enquiries, show project comments, operate site features, and maintain or protect the website. It is not sold. Site hosting, database and any enabled AI service may process information as needed to provide those functions.

Retention and your choices

Messages and comments may be retained for as long as they are useful for responding, displaying comments, or maintaining the site. To ask about or request deletion of information you submitted, email subha@bluconnetmedia.com.

Third-party links and security

This portfolio may link to external websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Reasonable care is taken to protect submitted information, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The latest version will be posted here. Questions about privacy can be sent to subha@bluconnetmedia.com.

This is a general website privacy notice, not legal advice. Please review it against the site’s actual hosting, analytics, AI and data-retention configuration before relying on it as a compliance document.
