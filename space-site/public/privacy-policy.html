01

Information you choose to share

If you use the contact form, the site may receive your name, email address and message so Subhadip can respond. If you leave a project comment, your name and comment text may be stored and displayed with that project. Please don’t include sensitive or confidential information.

02

Contact, comments & Space Bot

Contact messages and comments are handled by the site’s configured backend when available. Space Bot uses the text you submit to generate a reply; depending on the active configuration, that request may be processed by the site server and an enabled AI service. Avoid sharing private information in chat.

When backend services are unavailable, some demo features may store information or image choices in your browser’s local storage. Browser-local demo messages are not delivered to Subhadip.

03

How information is used

Information is used to reply to enquiries, display project comments, provide site features, and maintain or protect the website. It is not sold. Hosting, database and enabled AI providers may process information only as needed to provide their services.

04

Storage & retention

Messages and comments may be retained while they are useful for responding, displaying comments or maintaining the site. Local-storage data remains in your browser until you clear this site’s data. The site does not promise a particular retention period.

05

Your choices

You can choose not to use the contact, comment or chat features. To ask about or request deletion of information you submitted, email subha@bluconnetmedia.com. External sites linked from this portfolio have their own privacy policies and practices.

06

Updates & contact

This notice may change as the site or its services change. The latest version will be published on this page. Privacy questions can be sent to subha@bluconnetmedia.com.

Important

This is a general privacy notice, not legal advice. It should be checked against the actual hosting, analytics, AI providers and data-retention setup before being relied on as a compliance document.
